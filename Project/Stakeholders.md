# *Stakeholder Analysis:*
| **Stakeholder** | **Role** | **Interest** / **Expectations** | **Influence on the System** | **How the System Affects the Stakeholder** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patients / Customers | The people who will actually use the system to request refills | Want it to be quick, simple, and reliable, and want to know the status of their order | High, the whole system is built around what makes refilling prescriptions easier for them | Saves them time and effort, though patients who aren't comfortable with technology might struggle to use it |
| Pharmacy Staff | The employees who will receive and process refill requests | Want the system to fit into how they already work without making things more complicated | High, order-processing and inventory features are shaped around what they need day to day | Changes their process from handling requests manually to using the system, so they will need some training |
| Pharmacy Management | Oversees whether the system gets used and pays for things like hosting | Wants the system to actually improve efficiency and be worth the cost | Medium to High, they control the budget and decide if the system gets adopted | Benefits from a more efficient process, but is responsible for the ongoing hosting costs |
| Our Team (Developers) | Responsible for designing, building, and testing the system | Wants to deliver something that works and meets the course requirements | High, we are the ones making the technical decisions | This is a learning experience for us, and our grade depends on how well we deliver it |
