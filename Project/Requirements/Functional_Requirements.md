# Functional Requirements  

## *Student 1 Contributions (Haya)*  
**FR-01**: The system shall allow a user to log in using valid credentials. *(Source: S-01, Patient)*  
**FR-02**: The system shall allow the patient to choose a prescription to refill. *(Source: S-01, Patient)*  
**FR-03**: The system shall allow staff to view details such as the patient, medication, and dosage. *(Source: S-02, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-04**: The system shall allow a logged-in patient to view the status of their submitted refill requests. *(Source: S-03, Patient)*  
**FR-05**: The system shall update the status of requests automatically. *(Source: S-03, Patient)*  

## *Student 2 Contributions (Dima)*  
**FR-06**: The system shall allow a doctor or a pharmacy staff member to create a new prescription record including medication name, dosage, & no. refills. *(Source: S-06, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-07**: The system shall allow a doctor to modify an existing prescription dosage or refill count if required change happens to the treatment. *(Source: S-06, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-08**: The system shall prevent a refill request from being submitted if the prescription has expired or has no more allowed refills. *(Source: S-01, Patient)*  
**FR-09**: The system shall allow only a pharmacy staff member to advance a refill request to the next stage in sequence. *(Source: S-07, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-10**: The system shall allow a staff member to filter or sort refill requests by their current stage. *(Source: S-07, Pharmacy Staff)*  

## *Student 3 Contributions (Maryam)*  
**FR-11**: The system shall allow a pharmacy staff member to reject a refill request and provide a reason (e.g., out of stock, prescription expired.) *(Source: S-02, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-12**: The system shall send a notification to the patient when their refill order status changes (approved, rejected, ready for pickup). *(Source: S-03, Patient)*  
**FR-13**: The system shall allow pharmacy staff to update medication inventory quantities when stock is received. *(Source: S-04, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-14**: The system shall automatically flag a medication as low stock when its quantity falls below a predefined threshold. *(Source: S-04, Pharmacy Management)*  
**FR-15**: The system shall allow a new patient to register an account by entering their name, contact information, and date of birth. *(Source: S-05, Patient)*  

## *Student 4 Contributions (Yuchang)*  
**FR-16**: The system shall allow a patient to view the details of their active prescriptions, including medication name, dosage, and remaining refills. *(Source: S-01/S-06, Patient)*  
**FR-17**: The system shall allow pharmacy staff to mark an approved refill request as ready for pickup once the medication has been prepared. *(Source: S-07, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-18**: The system shall allow a patient to view the pharmacy pickup location associated with their refill request. *(Source: S-03, Patient)*  
**FR-19**: The system shall allow pharmacy staff to view the details of a selected refill request, including the patient, medication, dosage, and request status. *(Source: S-02, Pharmacy Staff)*  
**FR-20**: The system shall record the date and time whenever a refill request changes status. *(Source: S-07, Pharmacy Staff)*
