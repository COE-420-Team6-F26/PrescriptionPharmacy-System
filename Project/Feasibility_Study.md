# *Initial Feasibility Study:*

## Technical Feasibility
The system can be developed using open-source, widely available software. Web development tools (frontend, backend, and database) can be used to build a web portal or application so patients can request orders and refills. No specialized hardware is required. Since the team has experience with these technologies, completing the system with our current skills is feasible.

## Economic Feasibility
Since the system mainly requires open-source tools, costs are not expected to be highly unreasonable. The main ongoing cost is cloud hosting. Required resources include access to the pharmacy's inventory system, a cloud hosting budget, and testing/training time for pharmacy staff. Limitations include a low adoption risk among older patients who are less comfortable with technology, and a downtime risk (system issues could delay patients receiving their medication on time).

## Operational Feasibility
The proposed system is expected to meet a genuine user need, as it simplifies and speeds up prescription refills for patients. Intended users are likely to use and accept the system, though older patients may be more skeptical due to discomfort with technology or limited digital literacy.

## Schedule Feasibility
The major project features and deliverables can realistically be completed within the available course duration, provided the work is planned carefully. After dividing responsibilities among team members and organizing a schedule, the project is achievable, though time constraints remain a risk if the project runs over.

## Overall Feasibility
Overall, the system appears feasible with a low level of risk. Key constraints include technological literacy among patients (especially older ones, who make up a large share of pharmacy customers), securing budget for cloud hosting, and meeting deadlines for each project activity.
